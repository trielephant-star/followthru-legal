FollowThru Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

FollowThru is a daily coach for people with a side hustle, with Momo the sloth. This policy explains what we keep, why, who sees it, and how to delete it. Short version: your data is yours, we don't sell it, we don't show ads, and you can delete everything inside the app.

What FollowThru keeps

On your phone (always): your goals and settings, your side hustle details (what it is, how far along, your 90-day goal and weekly hours), your daily One Thing, your Power Hour sessions, your Ship Log, your parked ideas, your ticks and counts for each day, your to-dos, your check-in answers, anything you write in the optional journal step, your streak and points. The app works fully with nothing leaving your phone.

In your account (only if you create one): the same information, so it survives a lost phone or a new device. To make an account you give us an email address; we email you a 6-digit code and keep no password. We also keep the name you tell Momo, if you shared it.

Where your account data lives

In a database run by Supabase, hosted in the United States (AWS, N. Virginia). Every row is locked so that only the signed-in person can read it. Email codes are sent by an email delivery provider on our behalf.

AI features (Claude by Anthropic)

AI features are off until you say yes, and only work if you have an account.

  • Morning plan: if you turn it on, each morning the names of your goals, how many days in the past week you finished each one, and the titles of to-dos due soon are sent to Claude, an AI system made by Anthropic, to write your focus and tip. Your journal and how you feel are not sent.
  • Evening reply: only when you tap "Hear what Momo thinks", tonight's check-in is sent to Claude to write Momo's reply. This includes your answers, how you feel, and anything you wrote in the journal step. It is sent only because you tapped the button.
  • Ask Momo (chat): only after you say yes, and only for signed-in users. When you send a message, it goes to Claude together with your recent chat messages, your side hustle details (type, stage, 90-day goal, weekly hours), the names of your daily goals, your open to-dos with deadlines, and today's One Thing. Your journal and how you feel are not sent. Momo can suggest changes to your to-dos and plan; nothing changes until you tap Apply. The chat history is kept on your phone only (you can clear it with "New chat"). Free accounts may have a daily limit on chats. If you write words that suggest a crisis, Momo shows the 988 Lifeline and does not send the message to the AI. Turn this off any time on the Me screen.

Anthropic processes this to produce the reply under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). We don't use your data to train AI models, and we don't ask Anthropic to. You can turn the morning plan off any time on the Me screen.

Anonymous usage stats (optional)

If you say yes (once, in the welcome, or any time on the Me screen), FollowThru sends anonymous events to PostHog, such as "started a Power Hour" with the number of minutes. They carry a random id made on your phone and the app version, and are not linked to your account. They never include your goals, One Things, ideas, names, journal or anything you typed. Turn it off any time on the Me screen and the random id is erased.

Feedback

The "Tell Momo" button saves your message, the screen you were on, the app version and your phone type to your account so the developer can read it. Don't put anything in there you wouldn't want read.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. They only appear if you allow notifications, and you can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no selling or renting your data.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No crash-reporting tools at this time, and no usage statistics unless you switch them on (see Anonymous usage stats above). If we add anything, this page changes first.
  • No medical, diagnostic or mental-health service. FollowThru is a habit coach, not a replacement for professional care.

If you're struggling

If something you write suggests you may be in crisis, the app shows the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988 in the US). Outside the US, please contact your local emergency number.

Deleting your data

  • Account and everything in it: Me, then Delete my account. This erases your account data and everything on that phone, and it can't be undone.
  • Just this phone, no account: deleting the app removes everything stored on it.
  • Can't open the app? Email helpme.trielephant@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days. More details on the delete your data page.

Messages we emailed you and security logs kept by our service providers may persist briefly under their own retention rules.

Children

FollowThru is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has an account, email us and we'll remove it.

Changes and contact

If we make a meaningful change, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app. Questions or requests: helpme.trielephant@gmail.com.
