Delete your FollowThru data

App: FollowThru (package com.usefollowthru.app) · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open FollowThru and go to Me.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This erases your account and everything stored in it (goals, to-dos, Ship Log, ideas, check-ins, journal, feedback you sent), and everything stored on that phone. It can't be undone.

No account?

If you never made an account, all your data is only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Can't open the app?

Email helpme.trielephant@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my FollowThru data". We will delete your account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and what may remain

  • Deleted: your account, your email address, and all content tied to it in our database.
  • Not stored by us: Ask Momo chat history stays on your phone only, so it goes when you delete the app or the account.
  • May remain briefly: security and delivery logs kept by our service providers under their own retention rules.
