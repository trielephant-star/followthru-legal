Meet Momo

A coach who remembers, and never guilt-trips.

Momo used to be the king of “tomorrow.” Now he helps builders actually ship.

  • Knows your project. Your goal, your stage, what got in your way last week, and when you actually have time.

Questions

Good to know

Is FollowThru free?

Yes, free during the beta, with everything included. Later there will be a free plan and an optional Pro plan with more from Momo. Beta testers will hear first.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone (through TestFlight while in beta) and Android (through Google Play testing). Join with the phone you use and we’ll send the right link.

How is this different from a to-do or habit app?

To-do apps let lists grow forever and habit apps punish a missed day. FollowThru asks for one meaningful Move a day for one project, and a coach who knows that project helps you do it.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing breaks. Momentum dips a little and comes right back with your next Move. Momo never guilt-trips. He helps you make tomorrow’s Move smaller if that’s what it takes.

What does Momo know about me?

Only what you tell him in the app: your project, your Moves and your chats. You can see what he remembers in Settings → What Momo knows, and delete any of it. Your data is never sold or used to train AI.

When do I get access?

We’re letting testers in in small groups. Join the beta and we’ll email you an invite with the link for your phone.
