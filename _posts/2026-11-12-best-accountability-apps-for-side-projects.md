The best accountability app for a side project is the one that fixes the specific thing that stops you. Focusmate gives you someone working beside you, Habitica turns tasks into a game, Finch supports a self-care routine, and a project coach gives you one step a day. A good friend works too.

If you're building something after work, you've probably noticed that nobody checks whether you showed up. No boss, no deadline, no one asking how it went. That is the gap an accountability tool is supposed to fill. (We make one of them: FollowThru is the accountability app for people building something of their own. We'll be fair about the others.)

The catch is that "accountability" means different things in different apps. Here is what each option does, who it suits, and how to pick without trying all of them.

- Pick the app that matches your actual problem: company, rewards, calm, or a clear next step
- Focusmate, Habitica and Finch are well-made tools with different jobs
- A human partner still beats an app for feeling seen
- Start with one option for two weeks, then add another only if a gap remains
{: .summary}

## What does "accountability" actually mean for a side project?

Four different things hide behind the word:

1. **Company.** Someone else is working at the same time, so you start.
2. **Rewards.** Finishing a task feels good right away, so you come back.
3. **Calm routine.** A gentle daily ritual that keeps you steady.
4. **Direction and follow-up.** Someone helps you choose the next step and asks how it went.

You probably struggle with one or two of these. Name yours first and the choice gets easy.

## Focusmate: someone working beside you

[Focusmate](https://www.focusmate.com) describes itself as "virtual coworking." You book a session, join a video call with a partner, say what you'll do, and both of you work quietly. It uses an idea called body doubling: working near someone else who is focused on their own task.

Sessions are 25, 50 or 75 minutes, and you join from your browser. Its site mentions a free tier with a limited number of sessions per week and a paid tier with more, so check their page for current plans.

**Pick it if** your trouble is starting. Having a person on camera makes sitting down much easier. **Look elsewhere if** your trouble is knowing what to work on: Focusmate doesn't choose the task for you.

## Habitica: your to-do list as a game

[Habitica](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habitica-gamified-taskmanager/id994882113) calls itself a "free habit-building and productivity app that uses retro RPG elements." You make an avatar, add tasks, and earn gold, experience and items when you check them off. It has dailies, habits, a to-do list, plus parties and challenges if you want to team up.

It's free to use with optional purchases, according to its App Store listing. It also has streak counters, which suit some people and feel heavy to others.

**Pick it if** small wins and game rewards motivate you. **Look elsewhere if** a broken streak makes you want to quit, or if you need help deciding which project task matters most.

## Finch: a self-care pet

[Finch](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/finch-self-care-pet/id1528595748) describes itself as a way to "take care of your pet by taking care of yourself." You do short self-care exercises, check in on your mood, and your virtual pet grows. Its listing also mentions a goal tracker, journaling prompts and breathing exercises.

It is built for wellbeing, not for shipping a project, and that's fine. A calmer routine can make everything else easier.

**Pick it if** you're running on empty and want a kind, low-pressure daily ritual. **Look elsewhere if** you need something that knows your project and its next step.

## FollowThru: one project step a day

Each day you pick one small Move (15, 30 or 60 minutes). Tap Start now and Momo, a friendly sloth coach, breaks it into steps with a timer. In the evening he checks in with a short chat and remembers your project. On Sundays you get a Weekly Builder Report.

Progress is measured as momentum rather than streaks, so a missed day doesn't reset anything. It's in free beta on iPhone (TestFlight) and Android (Google Play testing), which means it's newer and less proven than the others here.

**Pick it if** your problem is "what do I do tonight, and did it help?" **Look elsewhere if** you mainly need live company, or a game, or a wellbeing app.

## What about a human accountability partner?

A person can notice you've gone quiet, and a friendly message from a real friend can mean more than any notification. We cover how to set one up in [how to find an accountability partner for your side project](/blog/accountability-partner-for-side-projects/).

Their weakness is availability. Real people get busy, and asking for help every week can feel awkward. That's why pairing a person with an app can work well.

## A simple way to choose in five minutes

Ask yourself what usually happens on a night you don't work on your project:

- "I sat down and stared at it": try Focusmate or a coach that picks the step.
- "It felt boring, so I did something else": try Habitica.
- "I was too drained to think": try Finch first, then add a project step.
- "I didn't know what to do next": try FollowThru, or write three next steps in a notebook.
- "No one would notice either way": ask a friend, and add an app.

Run your pick for two weeks. If you're still missing something, add a second tool.

Pick the tool you will still open on a tired Wednesday. Skip the one that looks the most impressive.
