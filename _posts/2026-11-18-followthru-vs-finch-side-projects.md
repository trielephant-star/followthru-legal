Finch is a self-care app where you look after a virtual pet by looking after yourself. FollowThru is a coach for one project: it gives you one small Move a day and checks in each evening. Pick Finch for daily self-care. Pick FollowThru if you want to finish something you are building.

If you build after work, you may have both on your phone, or be wondering whether one app could do both jobs. They sit close together because both are friendly, both use a character, and both go easy on you. But they point at different targets.

This post compares them fairly. (We make FollowThru: it is the accountability app for people building something of their own. Finch is made by someone else, and we only describe what we saw on its own pages.)

- Finch is about self-care: mood check-ins, journaling and a pet that grows
- FollowThru is about one project: a small Move a day and an evening check-in
- Finch helps you look after yourself; FollowThru helps you decide what to build tonight
- They fit together: care for yourself first, then do one small Move

## What is Finch good at?

Finch's own site describes it as ["your new self-care best friend"](https://finchcare.com). The idea is that you take care of your pet by taking care of yourself.

According to its [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/finch-self-care-pet/id1528595748), the app includes morning mood check-ins, evening gratitude sessions, a goal tracker, guided bullet journaling, guided breathing, a mood tracker with trends, and sleep routines. The listing says it is free with in-app purchases, so check Finch's page for current plans.

It is built for the kind of day where the win is "I looked after myself." If you are tired, stressed or rebuilding a routine, that is a very good place to start. Rest is part of any plan.

## What is FollowThru good at?

FollowThru starts from a different question: you know what you want to build, but not what to do tonight.

Each day you pick one small **Move** (15, 30 or 60 minutes). Tap **Start now** and **Momo**, a friendly sloth coach, breaks it into steps with a timer, drafts writing with you, asks sharp questions when you are deciding, and can write the prompt for your AI tool.

Momo checks in each evening with a short chat and remembers your project. Every Sunday you get a Weekly Builder Report: what you shipped, a pattern Momo noticed, and next week's focus with three Moves ready. Progress is **momentum, not streaks**, so a missed day does not reset anything.

It is in free beta, so it is newer and less proven than a long-running app. It is also built for one project at a time, and it is not a wellbeing or journaling app.

## Where do they differ most?

**Looking after you vs moving a project.** Finch's tasks are about your mood, sleep and routine. FollowThru's Moves are about the thing you are building, such as "write the first three lines of the product description."

**A pet that grows vs a coach that remembers.** Finch's pet grows as you complete self-care. Momo does not grow. He remembers your project from one day to the next, so the evening chat can ask about the shop photos you skipped on Monday.

**General goals vs a next step.** Finch has a goal tracker, which is useful for personal goals. A big project goal like "launch my shop" still needs breaking into steps. A Move is that step, already chosen.

**Wellbeing features vs shipping features.** Finch's listing mentions quizzes, insights and breathing. FollowThru has a Ship Log of every Move and milestone, and a weekly report on what you finished.

## Which one fits your situation?

- **Someone worn out after a long week:** Finch. A calm self-care routine may be what you need before any project work.
- **A mug shop owner** who never gets to the photos: FollowThru can pick "photograph three mugs against a plain wall" as the Move.
- **A YouTube cooking channel** that keeps stalling on editing: FollowThru's Move: "cut the first two minutes of the pasta video."
- **An app maker** stuck at 80%: FollowThru's Moves and Momo's prompts turn "finish the app" into something testable.
- **Someone who wants a daily journal and mood check-in:** Finch.

## Can you use both?

Yes, and they fit neatly. Here is one way to run a day:

1. Morning: do your self-care routine in Finch, if that helps you.
2. In the afternoon, pick tonight's Move: one small sentence with a verb and a finish.
3. Do the Move, with Momo's steps and timer if you like.
4. Let the evening check-in close the day.
5. On Sunday, read the weekly report and look at the three Moves ready for next week.

If that is too much, try one app for two weeks and ask: "Did I make progress on my project? If not, what was missing: energy, a next step or a reminder?" Add the second app only when you can name the gap. A quiet week does not mean the experiment failed.

For more on picking a small enough step, see [one small step a day](/blog/one-small-step-a-day-finish-what-you-start/). For a wider look at tools, see [the best accountability apps for side projects](/blog/best-accountability-apps-for-side-projects/).

If your energy is low, shrink the Move rather than skipping the day: "open the file and write one sentence" still counts, and it keeps the project warm.
